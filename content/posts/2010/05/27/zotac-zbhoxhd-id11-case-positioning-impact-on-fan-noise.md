As I was testing the ID11, I noticed differences in the thermal behavior based on how the case was positioned.   
I tested three positions; case open, case vertical, and case horizontal.

This is the fifth post in a [series of posts related to the Zotac ZBOX ZBOXHD-ID11](/2010/05/zotac-zbox-mini-pc-zboxhd-id11.html).

Summary:

- Place the case in a vertical position for best cooling.

I used the [Beta BIOS](/2010/05/zotac-zboxhd-id11-beta-bios-reduces-fan.html) for testing. I let the system sit idle, placed it under load, then back to idle, while I recorded the fan speed and temperatures. The ambient temperature was 21C / 70F.

From the data we can see that the fan does not appear to have sufficient ventilation, and that in the horizontal position the air flow appears to be severely restricted.

I am tempted to mod the case to allow for better airflow, maybe cut a larger opening for the intake, or replace the centrifugal blower fan with a conventional fan, something like the [Scythe KAZE JYU SLIM](http://www.scythe-usa.com/product/acc/035/sy1012sl12_detail.html).
