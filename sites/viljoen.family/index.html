Numbered in the South African de Villiers/Pama system: a is the stamvader, b1 to b6 his children, and b3c4 the fourth child of b3. The Viljoen name was carried forward through the lines of Henning and Johannes. Dates come from published descendant lists and are still to be verified against the Familiebond's Familieregister.

OriginsOorsprong

The Viljoens of South Africa descend from François Villion, a French Huguenot from Clermont who reached the Cape of Good Hope in 1671, well before the main Huguenot settlement of 1688.

In 1676 he married Cornelia Campenaar of Middelburg in the Netherlands, and the couple farmed near Stellenbosch. Their six children were baptized in Cape Town and Stellenbosch between 1677 and 1689, and over the generations the French name Villion settled into its Afrikaans form, Viljoen.

The ArmsDie familiewapen

Gules, a saltire Or. Mantling Or and Gules. Crest: a pair of eagle's wings folded Or.

A gold St Andrew's cross on red, derived from the arms of the French family Villon de Varennes. Their crest was a golden mural crown. At the Viljoen Festival of 1976 the Viljoen Familiebond voted to replace it with the pair of golden eagle's wings shown above.
